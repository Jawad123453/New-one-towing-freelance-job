Sample projectNeuroKit2HRV

Batch ECG → HRV pipeline: 40 subjects, young vs elderly

One script turns 40 raw ECG recordings (PhysioNet Fantasia, 250 Hz) into cleaned R-peaks, per-subject RR-interval files, a signal-quality score, and time- and frequency-domain HRV, followed by a corrected group comparison.

Sample projectValidationMIT-BIH

Does the R-peak detector actually work? Benchmarked against cardiologists

Before trusting any HRV number, I check the beat detection. Three open-source detectors were scored against expert-annotated beats in all 48 MIT-BIH Arrhythmia recordings (±150 ms window, the AAMI EC57 convention).

  • 109,494
    reference beats checked
  • 0.993
    best overall F1 (Pan-Tompkins)
  • 12 / 48
    records where detectors disagree, each explained

Finding: no detector wins everywhere. The popular NeuroKit2 default came second overall, and each detector breaks on different arrhythmias. That's why I validate on a labelled subset of your data before running the full study.
